# The Mechanism

The Mechanism (TM) is the primary research tool for my next monograph, *Infrastructural Inflections: Critical Technical Practice and the Architecture of Knowledge*, and will be critically examined in that book. The system is built from found infrastructure: an existing desktop and a decommissioned Raspberry Pi cluster, with a few pieces added where necessary. The project began as an exercise in critical technical practice. My goal was to learn about the affordances and constraints of AI for humanities and social science research by engaging in system design and maintenance myself, not paying for online services where I would be one of many customers doubling as product testers, or renting cloud infrastructure. In the parlance of my 2026 book [*Digital Modernity: Why We Need to Think Historically About the Digital Age*](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/oa-mono/10.4324/9781003684398/digital-modernity-james-smithies), TM is an example of 'minimally industrialized cognition'. This website provides high-level documentation intended to convey my motivations and design principles rather than detailed system information.
